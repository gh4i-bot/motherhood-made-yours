One-to-one coaching with Kacy

This is a soft place to land.

Real conversation about where you are and where you want to be.

No judgment, no rushing, no ten-step program. We slow down, we tell the truth, and we make room for the life you're actually wanting.

My process is simple. We start with a chat - thirty minutes, just to meet each other. If it feels right, we keep going from there, at a pace that fits your real life (nap schedules included).

What changed when they worked with Kacy

Amy Kochek

After being laid off from what she believed was her dream job, Amy felt angry, stuck and unsure what came next. Working with Kacy, she stopped trying to fix every outside problem and began finding peace and stability within herself. She saw changes in her relationships, career and mindset, but the change that mattered most was feeling connected to herself again.

“I feel like who I am, it hasn't changed. It's just I'm back home.”

Abby Kochek

For at least 10 years, Abby had felt stuck: ready for change and afraid of it at the same time. Working with Kacy helped her notice her blind spots, reframe old patterns and see that her past did not have to define what came next. In a space where she felt safe and supported, she began moving forward with practical ways to meet stress and change differently.

“I always feel validated, I feel seen, I feel truly heard.”

Jennifer Muscatel

Jennifer came to Kacy with an issue involving a colleague. In their work together, Kacy brought a subconscious pattern to light, and Jennifer realized it mattered more than the issue she had started with. The work shifted her focus from the conflict itself to what it was revealing beneath the surface.

“I have worked with therapists my whole life, since I was 12... in the two times I've worked with Kacy, I have had breakthroughs and aha moments that were quite remarkable.”
